Tally privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Tally is a grocery budget app. You keep a running total in the store, and the prices you scan help build a shared picture of what groceries cost at local stores. This page explains, in plain language, what Tally collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version:

  • Tally collects the prices you scan and your grocery trips, so your total is right and so shoppers can see real local prices.
  • Other shoppers can never see who scanned a price, your trips, or your budget.
  • Tally does not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools.
  • You can ask for all your data to be deleted at any time.

What Tally collects

Prices you scan or type in. For each one: the item's name and barcode, the price, any deal (like BOGO or "3 for $5"), the store chain, the county, the store's approximate location (rounded to about 100 meters, so it identifies the store, not you), the store's street and city, and the time. Prices from a receipt you scan are saved the same way.

Your finished trips. The store, its approximate location, the items in your cart, Tally's estimated total, the receipt total you entered or scanned, and your trip target if you set one. These are saved on our servers too. Moving your trips to a new phone isn't possible yet; it's coming with sign-in.

Photos of shelf tags and receipts. These are sent to our server to be read, and the photo is not stored. We keep a short record of each read (whether it worked, how long it took, what it cost us, and the price it read from a shelf tag) to measure how well reading works. Receipt records don't include item names.

Your location, only while you use the app, and only if you allow it. It's used to recognize which store you're in and which county you're in (for sales tax). Your phone turns the location into an address; Tally saves only the store's rounded location and street with your trip and its prices. Tally does not track your location in the background. If you say no, everything still works; you just pick your store yourself.

Your account. When you first open Tally, it creates an anonymous account: a random ID, with no name, email or phone number. This is how your data is kept separate from everyone else's.

What stays only on your phone

Your first name (for the greeting), your monthly budget, your shopping list and the trip in progress are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to our servers. Deleting the app deletes them.

Early-access email list

If you sign up for early access on our website, we keep your email address, the time you signed up, and which page you signed up from. We use it only to tell you about Tally (for example, when you can try it), never to sell or share. Each email has a link to leave the list, or email us and we'll remove you. The website doesn't use cookies or tracking tools.

Why we collect it

  • To make the app work: your running total, sales tax, deals, trip history and monthly spending.
  • To build shared local prices: prices from all shoppers are combined so Tally can show what an item costs at a store near you. When we show a price, we show the product, store, price and date, never who scanned it.
  • To improve Tally: for example, how often shelf-tag reading fails and what it costs.

Who can see your data

  • Other shoppers: never your trips, budget, list, name, or which prices you scanned. Shared prices are shown without any link to you.
  • The Tally team: we can see the stored data to run and fix the app. We don't look at individual people's data unless we need to, for example to fix a problem you reported.
  • We never sell your data and never share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Your trips are kept until you delete your account. Shelf-tag and receipt photos are not kept at all.

Prices are different: they're part of the shared picture of local grocery prices. When you delete your account, the prices you scanned stay in that shared data, but your account is removed from them, so nobody (including us) can tell they came from you.

Deleting your data

Email christiantallyapp@gmail.com from any address. Because accounts are anonymous, include the account ID shown at the bottom of the budget screen so we can find yours (it appears once Tally has saved a trip or a price). Within 30 days we'll delete your account, your trips and the records of your shelf-tag and receipt reads, remove your account from the prices you scanned (see above), and confirm by email. A "Delete my account" button inside the app is coming soon.

Children

Tally is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For big changes, we'll tell you in the app first.
